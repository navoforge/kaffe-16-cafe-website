01 — A little about us

Room to reconnect.

A neighbourhood café with a softer pace, tucked into the familiar rhythm of Kuala Lumpur.

This concept brings together the character of a city shophouse and the ease of a garden corner: textured brick, changing light, good company and time enough to enjoy it.
